# ADR-002 — Acceptance of the name "Kairos" and alternatives considered

**Date:** 2026-08-09
**Status:** Accepted

---

## Decision

The family is named **Kairos** (Greek: the opportune moment, distinct from
Chronos, sequential time).

---

## Rationale

The name describes what the family does: not measure time, but find the right
moment within it. The Govern brother (Brother 04) makes a per-sample decision
about what reaches the human and when — this is precisely the Kairos operation.

The name also distinguishes the family's purpose from a clock (Chronos) and
from a planner (which deals with future time). Kairos is about the present
moment that matters, which aligns with the Govern brother's role.

The name is semantically aligned with the core operation and concise enough for
internal technical use. This ADR records a conceptual naming decision only; it
does not claim trademark availability, market distinctiveness, or endorsement
by any client or third party.

---

## Alternatives considered

**Arbiter / Arbiter family:** Emphasizes the decision-making role but loses
the temporal dimension entirely. The family is organized *around* time; a
name that drops that would be misleading.

**Chronos family:** The opposite problem — describes sequential time, not
the opportune moment. Confuses the family's purpose.

**Sentinel:** Available, attention-management adjacent, but implies surveillance
rather than governance. Rejected for client-context reasons.

**SAGE (Sensory Attention Governance Engine):** Acronym-based. Considered for
the Govern brother alone (where it was accurate). Rejected as a family name
because it only describes one brother's function.

---

## Known tension

The name "Kairos" as applied to the full family is slightly aspirational:
Brothers 01–03 are world-facing (they don't directly find the human-relevant
moment). Only Brother 04 (Govern) directly performs the Kairos operation.

Accepted because:
1. The family's *output* is governed by the Kairos operation (Brother 04)
2. Brothers 01–03 exist to enable Brother 04's decision
3. Naming the family after its defining output is a common and defensible
   convention (e.g., a "search engine" family is named for what it delivers,
   not for each internal component)

## Commercial-name limitation

No trademark or commercial-name clearance was performed as part of this
technical ADR. Before using Kairos as a public product or client-facing brand,
the owner must perform the appropriate legal and market search in the intended
jurisdictions. Until then, the acceptance applies only to the repository's
internal architecture name.

---

## Review trigger

Revisit before client-facing commercialization, if an English-only name is
required, or if a proper clearance search identifies a conflict.
